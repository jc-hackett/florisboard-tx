SovereignBoard: what's new

For clinicians who demand that sovereignty. Newest first. Updates arrive in the app under Customization, then Keyboard updates.

3 October 2026

  • New name and icon: SovereignBoard.
  • Thumbs-down: select text and choose "Mark as bad", or use the thumbs-down under the three dots. Marked examples go to Recent edits for tuning.
  • Your own words come first in suggestions ("sov" offers SovereignBoard).
  • Smarter tapping: a tap near the edge of a key goes to the letter that fits the word. Bigger key targets.
  • Auto-cleanup after any sentence-ending mark, not just a double space.
  • One-tap setup links and a "Getting started" checklist for new people.
  • Emoji search fixed: tapping a result now inserts it.

2 October 2026

  • Dictation arrives already cleaned up, the same as the sparkle button.
  • The sparkle uses a stronger AI model; names stay hidden from it.
  • Emoji search; settings renamed "Customization" and moved to the top.
  • Recent edits: before and after for each cleanup, with notes (optional, on your phone only).
  • Choose your Enter, mic and sparkle colours.
  • Clearer messages when the sparkle can't help.

1 October 2026

  • The sparkle button: cleans up the selection, or the whole box. Tap again to undo.
  • Say punctuation: "comma", "question mark", "new paragraph", "quote … unquote".
  • Word suggestions, autocorrect, and a word list for names and terms.
  • No more clipboard pop-ups after dictating.

30 September 2026

  • Dictation streams while you talk: text arrives a moment after you stop.
  • Updates install from inside the app.
  • Tap the mic while it waits to cancel.
