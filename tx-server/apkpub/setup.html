Set up SovereignBoard

About five minutes, on your Android phone. Do the steps in order.

  1. Install the keyboard

    Tap below. Your browser will say the file "might be harmful": that's normal for any app that isn't from the Play Store, so tap Download anyway. Then open the downloaded file, allow installs from your browser when Android asks, and tap Install.

    Download the keyboard

  2. Turn it on

    Open the new app (SovereignBoard). It walks you through turning the keyboard on. Then, while typing anywhere, tap the small keyboard icon at the bottom of the screen and choose it.

  3. Connect it

    Come back to this page and tap the button. It fills in your personal access, so there's nothing to type.

    Connect my keyboard

    This link is missing its personal part. Ask Jeremiah for your setup link again.

  4. Allow the microphone

    The keyboard's settings open on "Customization". Tap "Getting started" until all three lines have a tick.
