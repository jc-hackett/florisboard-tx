SovereignBoard: what happens to your words

Plain-language summary, October 2026. Questions: ask Jeremiah Hackett, who runs the server.

Typing

Everything you type stays on your phone. Autocorrect, suggestions and smarter tapping all work on the phone itself.

Dictation

  • Only while the mic is on, your voice streams over an encrypted connection to a private server Jeremiah runs (hosted by Vultr, under a signed health-privacy agreement).
  • It is turned into text there and the audio is discarded straight away. Audio is never saved.
  • The server keeps only counts: when, how many words, how many seconds. Never what you said.

AI cleanup (the sparkle, and the tidy-up after dictation or a sentence)

  • Names, places, dates and numbers are swapped for placeholders on the server before the text goes to Anthropic's Claude for proofreading, and put back afterwards.
  • Anthropic processes it under a signed health-privacy agreement (BAA) with Jeremiah's company.
  • The server keeps no copy of the text.

On your phone

  • "Recent edits" (off unless you switch it on) and anything you mark 👎 are kept on your phone only, and wiped after seven days.
  • Nothing is sent while the keyboard is in incognito mode, and password fields are never touched.

If you use it with your own clients

Jeremiah's agreements cover his own practice. If you dictate client information, talk to him first about the agreement you'd need.
